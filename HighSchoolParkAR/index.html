Site History

Constructed in 1904 at the corner of Montgomery Avenue and High School Road, Cheltenham High School served as a premier educational facility with subsequent additions built in the 1920s and 30s.

After a new high school was built in Wyncote in 1959, the building continued its educational lifespan as Ogontz Junior High until it was retired and abandoned by the mid-1980s. Years of disrepair and vandalism culminated in a spectacular fire that gutted the main structure.

On July 3, 1995, the remains of the building were demolished and imploded. Following the demolition, the 11-acre site was transformed into High School Park to preserve the native ecosystem where the school once stood.
