Collective Futures

Perturbations Workshop & Performance

On October 25, 2026, at 11am at the Asian Arts Initiative, Sam Cusumano and Raúl Romero presented "Perturbations" as part of the Collective Futures Parallel Play Symposium. The session explored the intersection of human perception and the invisible changes happening in organic systems like plants. By extracting complex information from living biological systems, the exhibition featured data analysis algorithms, visualization methods for plant music, and physical perturbations that allowed live plants to actuate and play a modified drum kit using actuators.
