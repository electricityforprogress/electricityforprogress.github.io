Collective Futures: Parallel Play

Perturbations

Presented by BYO Print

An exploration and examination of Biodata Sonification with Raúl Romero and Sam Cusumano.

Hosted on October 25th, 2026, at the Asian Arts Initiative, this free workshop and performance delved into the intersection of human perception and the invisible changes happening in organic systems. The music you are listening to is being generated in real-time by three plants.

Through our four-part process—Introduction, Amplification, Explication, and Interrogation—we extract complex information from living biological systems. By translating real-time microcurrent fluctuations into MIDI data, we employ data analysis algorithms, visualization methods, and physical perturbations to allow live plants to actuate and play a modified physical drum kit.
